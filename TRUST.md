# What you have to trust

**Status (2026-10-21): work in progress.** The main theorem is not assembled yet; see PLAN.md.

## Axioms

`lake env lean Axioms.lean` prints the axioms of the main theorem and of its ingredients. The target is Lean's three
standard axioms `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`, no `sorry`, no `axiom` declaration and no `native_decide`.

## The statement

The kernel checks the proof. It cannot check that the statement means what we claim, so read these definitions:

| file | definition |
|---|---|
| `ZetaIrrational/Statement.lean` | `OneOfZeta7To21 := ∃ j ∈ ({7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, 19, 21} : Finset ℕ), Irrational (riemannZeta j).re` |

`riemannZeta` and `Irrational` are Mathlib's. Mathlib's `zeta_nat_eq_tsum_of_gt_one` identifies `riemannZeta j` with
`∑' n, 1/n^j`; `ZetaIrrational/Defs.lean` (`riemannZeta_re_eq_zetaR`) turns this into the real series used internally.
